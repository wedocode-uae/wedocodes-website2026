FAQs

Common questions about WeDoCodes solutions.

Can you build software for our exact workflow?

Yes. The system can be planned around your users, approvals, records, reports and operating steps.

Can you modernize an Excel or manual process?

Yes. Existing Excel structures, forms and manual tracking can be converted into a controlled software workflow.

Can the software work on mobile and desktop?

Yes. Web apps, dashboards and portals can be made responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Can you connect WhatsApp, payment gateway or email?

Yes. Integrations can be planned for notifications, payment flows, emails, SMS, WhatsApp and third-party services where supported.

Can you build cloud-based and local systems?

Yes. The deployment model can be selected based on project needs, access requirements and budget.

Can access be limited by employee role?

Yes. Role-based access can control who can view, create, approve, edit, export or delete records.

Can reports be exported to PDF or Excel?

Yes. Dashboards, summaries, printable reports, PDF exports and Excel exports can be included.

Can you provide support after launch?

Yes. Support and improvement can continue after deployment based on the selected engagement model.
